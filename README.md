StudyMate – AI-Powered Study Assistant

A UI/UX case study for StudyMate, an AI-powered study assistant designed to make studying more organized and less frustrating for students. The idea is to bring useful study tools into one place so students can manage their subjects, ask questions, understand difficult concepts, and keep track of their progress without constantly switching between different apps.

 Figma Prototype: https://www.figma.com/proto/SCDvZukOVFb0lvKhsjYOlT/StudyMate-Project?node-id=0-1&t=PRBGj3vj6Z2M5hfJ-1

 Overview

The Problem

Students often use several different apps while studying. They might use one app for notes, another for searching for explanations, another for tracking their tasks, and another for asking questions.

Switching between all these tools can make studying feel more complicated than it needs to be. I wanted to explore whether these common study needs could be brought together into one simple and easy-to-use experience.

The Idea

StudyMate is designed as a central study companion where students can organize their subjects, ask an AI assistant questions, get explanations when they are stuck, and keep track of their study progress.

The main goal was not to create another complicated productivity app, but to design something that feels simple enough to use regularly while studying.

Design Process

I worked through the design process from the early stages of research and ideation to wireframing and the final high-fidelity prototype.

The process included:

- Research and problem identification
- Comparative analysis
- User persona
- Ideation and sketching
- User flow
- Low-fidelity wireframes
- High-fidelity UI design
- Interactive Figma prototype

The final result is an interactive Figma prototype that demonstrates how a student could use StudyMate as part of their everyday study routine.
